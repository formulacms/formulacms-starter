# Community access

ADR-0053 defines the accepted fresh Community target: one durable installation
owner with one fixed code-defined `admin` role. Ownership and role identity
remain distinct.

Fresh Community has no additional CMS members, invitations, Editor/Viewer
roles, custom roles, or configurable permission policies. Preserved legacy
member, invite, role, seat, workflow, and entitlement shapes are compatibility
and migration evidence; they are not fresh Community authority.

A later Team extension may add members, roles, permissions, and collaboration
through reviewed seams while preserving the owner and fixed Admin contract.
